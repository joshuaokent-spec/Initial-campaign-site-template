Get in Touch

Government for People, Not Power. Practical Reform for Public Good. A campaign built around reform has to be willing to listen. Whether you have a question, a concern, a policy idea, a press request, or want to get involved, this page is here to help you connect directly with the campaign.

Send a Message

Use the form below to contact the campaign about platform questions, event information, volunteer opportunities, media inquiries, public concerns, or broader reform ideas. This campaign is rooted in public accountability, and that begins with being reachable.

What can you contact the campaign about?

Community members are encouraged to reach out about democratic reform, anti-corruption ideas, labor and wage concerns, housing and cost-of-living issues, education policy, public safety and justice reform, infrastructure, public systems, accessibility, event requests, or ways the campaign can better engage with people across Michigan.

Why public contact matters

A campaign centered on accountability should not hide behind slogans or distance itself from the public. Listening to people directly is part of what makes reform credible. Public trust is rebuilt when institutions, leaders, and campaigns are willing to hear criticism, answer questions, and remain open to real dialogue.

Best Way to Reach the Campaign

The form on this page is now the primary contact path for general questions, accessibility needs, event suggestions, volunteer interest, and press requests. That keeps outreach in one place and avoids half-published contact details.

Response Window

General inquiries are typically reviewed within 2 to 3 business days. Time-sensitive event or press requests should still use the form, but choosing the right subject line helps the campaign route them faster.

Press & Media

Members of the press can request interviews, statements, background, or policy clarification here. For published campaign messaging and longer statements, visit the Press page.
