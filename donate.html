Why Contribute

Contributions help transform ideas into public action. This campaign is built around reform that serves workers, students, families, and communities rather than the comfort of entrenched power. That message only reaches people when there are resources to organize, communicate, travel, host events, print materials, and keep the campaign visible in public life.

A contribution is not only financial support. It is also a vote of confidence in a people-first politics that takes democratic accountability, anti-corruption reform, housing stability, educational dignity, and public service seriously.

Where Contributions Go

Support helps fund the practical work of running a public campaign. That includes outreach materials, event preparation, volunteer support, transportation, digital communications, website tools, printed literature, signs, and the day-to-day work of helping more people hear the campaign’s message.

Contributions also help strengthen the campaign’s ability to respond to questions, share policy priorities, coordinate supporters, and maintain a visible public presence across Lansing and beyond.

$10

Helps support flyers, printed handouts, and small outreach materials used at events and public-facing campaign efforts.

$25

Helps strengthen volunteer coordination, communications, and local organizing support.

$50+

Helps expand outreach, improve campaign readiness, and build a more visible reform-focused presence.

Why Small Donations Matter

Small donations matter because they show a campaign is being supported by ordinary people rather than relying only on wealthy backers or insulated networks. A campaign grounded in the public should be powered by the public whenever possible.

Even modest support adds up. A large number of small contributions can help create independence, momentum, and proof that the campaign’s message resonates beyond elite circles.

Grassroots Support

This campaign is strongest when it grows from the ground up. Grassroots support means people are not just observing politics from a distance. They are helping shape it. That support may come through donations, volunteering, turnout at events, sharing campaign information, and direct engagement with the issues.

A grassroots campaign carries a different kind of legitimacy. It reflects people who believe public life should be rebuilt around dignity, accountability, and common good, not organized around elite access and private capture.

A Contribution to People-First Reform

This campaign is built around stronger democracy, anti-corruption safeguards, labor dignity, housing stability, educational opportunity, and durable public systems that serve ordinary people. Supporting the campaign means helping those ideas enter public life with real force and not remain trapped in private frustration or unfinished policy documents.

Secure Processing

Contributions are intended to be processed through Stripe Checkout. In this upgraded demo, the donate buttons call the included checkout endpoint directly so the site behaves honestly: live when the backend is configured, explanatory when it is not.

Campaign Finance / Transparency Note

This website is currently being developed as a campaign and portfolio project. Before accepting real political contributions, all applicable campaign finance rules, reporting requirements, disclosure obligations, and donation compliance standards should be reviewed and followed.

A campaign that speaks about transparency should also take compliance and donor accountability seriously. That means building donation systems that are not only secure, but also lawful, reviewable, and honest about how support is handled.

Other Ways to Help

Financial support matters, but there are many ways to strengthen the campaign. You can volunteer, attend events, share the campaign with others, ask questions, and help bring a deeper reform conversation into public life.
