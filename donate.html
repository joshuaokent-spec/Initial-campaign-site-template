Why contributions matter

Campaigns rely on community support to fund outreach materials, event preparation, volunteer coordination, digital communications, and voter contact efforts. Donations help ensure the campaign can continue reaching residents with clear information and meaningful opportunities to engage.

$10 Contribution

Helps support printed handouts, flyers, and small outreach materials used at campaign events and community gatherings.

$25 Contribution

Supports volunteer efforts, neighborhood outreach, and the tools needed to stay connected with voters.

$50+ Contribution

Helps expand communications, event readiness, and the campaign’s ability to reach more people across the community.

Secure Donation Processing

For a live campaign website, donations would be processed through a secure third-party platform such as Stripe, PayPal, ActBlue, or another hosted payment provider. Using a trusted processor helps protect donor information and ensures transactions are handled safely and professionally.

Transparency and Trust

Supporters deserve to know how their contributions help. Donations strengthen campaign communication, public outreach, volunteer coordination, and the ability to share a clear message with the community. A strong grassroots campaign is built by many people giving what they can and participating in ways that matter.
