Join the Team

Campaigns are built by people who care enough to show up, speak out, and help their community. Volunteers make it possible to connect with residents, organize events, and keep the campaign moving forward.

Ways to Get Involved

Whether you have a few hours a week or can only help occasionally, there is a place for you on the campaign.

Canvassing

Speak directly with voters in neighborhoods, share campaign information, and help build stronger local connections.

Phone Banking

Reach supporters and community members by phone to share updates, answer questions, and encourage participation.

Event Support

Help with set-up, greeting guests, organizing materials, and making campaign events welcoming and well-run.

Why volunteer?

Volunteering is one of the most meaningful ways to support a campaign. It helps build momentum, strengthens outreach, and creates more opportunities for real conversation with the community. Every volunteer contribution matters, whether it happens behind the scenes or out in the field.

Volunteer Sign-Up

Fill out the form below to let the campaign know how you would like to help.

What happens next?

After signing up, a member of the campaign team can follow up with more information about volunteer needs, event opportunities, and the best next steps based on your interests and availability.

Other ways to support

Not ready to volunteer yet? You can still support the campaign by attending an event, sharing campaign information with others, or making a contribution to help expand outreach efforts.
